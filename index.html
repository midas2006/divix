How it works

A broken system creates a perfect opportunity.

When borrowers default, banks take their land but are legally pressured to sell it fast at recovery price, not market value. That gap is where Divix lives.

01
Bank seizes distressed land

When a borrower defaults on a loan secured by land, the bank forecloses and takes ownership. The land becomes REO, Real Estate Owned, and the bank is legally required to dispose of it as quickly as possible.

Regulated by US law
02
Bank sells at recovery price

Banks don't want land. They want their loan amount back. So they sell at whatever price recovers their outstanding loan, not the actual market value. This creates a structurally guaranteed discount on every single distressed property.

Always below market value
03
Divix packages it into fractions

Divix acquires or partners on the distressed property, places it inside a Special Purpose Vehicle, and divides it into fractions starting at $25. Each fraction is priced at the bank's recovery price, which is always below what the land is actually worth.

SPV legal structure
04
You invest. You're instantly in profit.

You buy a fraction starting at $25. Because the total price of all fractions equals the bank's recovery price, which is below market value, your fraction is already worth more than you paid the moment the transaction completes.

Legal ownership

Your ownership is real, legally recorded, and protected.

Every property on Divix is held inside a Special Purpose Vehicle, a dedicated legal entity created solely to hold that one piece of land. Your fraction is a legally documented share in that entity. If Divix ever ceased to exist, your ownership remains intact.

1
Property is identified and verified

Divix sources distressed REO land from bank databases, verifies title history, tax records, and confirms the discount between recovery price and market value.

2
SPV is created for this property

A dedicated Special Purpose Vehicle, a legal LLC, is formed specifically to hold this one property. It owns nothing else. This protects investors from any other Divix liabilities.

3
Fractions go on sale from $25

The SPV divides into fractions listed on the Divix platform. Investors purchase fractions and become legally recorded shareholders of the SPV, which owns the land.

4
Property funds, ownership locks in

Once all fractions are sold, ownership is legally recorded for every investor. Funds held in escrow are only released to acquire the property once fully funded.

5
Hold, grow, or sell on marketplace

Track your portfolio value as the land appreciates. After the minimum holding period, sell your fraction to other investors on the Divix secondary marketplace at current market value.
